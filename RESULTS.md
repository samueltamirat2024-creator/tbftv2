# Evaluation matrix -- harness output

Generated by `vdr.bench.Bench`. n = 4, f = 1, 2 runs per configuration.
Warm-up 1500 ms, steady window 3000 ms. Operating points are re-measured and stepped down until the merged p99 is
within 500 ms; the path taken is in each row's notes.

> **Provenance warning. These are not measurements of a BFT system.**
> They were produced against the in-process simulated total-order layer
> (`vdr.replication.SimulatedCluster`), not against BFT-SMaRt on a real cluster.
>
> The latency figures are dominated by two **hardcoded constants** in
> `SimulatedCluster.standard()` -- 3000 us charged per consensus instance and 300 us
> for a single-replica round trip -- chosen to match DepSpace's 2008 Emulab anchor
> points. Those constants are inputs to this run, not results of it. A p50 derived
> from them tells you what the harness was told to assume, not what a cluster does.
>
> What these rows DO establish: the arrival schedule, warm-up, merged-histogram
> percentiles, per-operation split, burst accounting and operating-point admission
> all work end to end, and the batching and fast-path mechanisms move the numbers in
> the direction the design predicts. That is milestone M0's question.
>
> They are NOT the numbers that belong in Paper 2, Table III. Fill that table from M9
> runs against the real engine, with both synthetic constants removed.
> Run with -Dvdr.replication=bftsmart once the cluster is up.
